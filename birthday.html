A letter written with care

Dear Kanishika,

I have thought about this more times than I can count. About how two people who meant so much to each other could end up on opposite sides of a silence neither of us chose.

Whatever happened between us — whatever words were said, or left unsaid — I am truly sorry. Not the kind of sorry that fills a gap, but the kind that comes from genuinely wishing things had gone differently.

You have always been someone who carries warmth naturally — the kind of person a room feels lighter around. I miss that. I miss you. The real you. My friend.

I am not asking you to forget. I am just asking for a chance to remind you that you still matter deeply — and that some friendships deserve to be kept, even when it takes courage to reach back for them.

With all the sincerity I have —

always yours, a friend ♡
